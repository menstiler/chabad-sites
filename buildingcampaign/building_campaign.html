From Home to Campus!

In 2023 we embarked on an ambitious journey to establish a permanent home for the Jewish community on the South Shore. Our recently acquired large estate home is already bustling with Chabad House programs and activities, demonstrating its immediate impact. Now, we're ready for the next phase: transforming this beautiful property into a full-fledged, state-of-the-art Campus for Jewish Life, that will serve as the heart of Jewish life for our growing community.
